    ⚖️ Weight tracking & forecast

    Your measured weight takes priority. JARVIS can make a rough projection using your recorded food calories, step calories, and an adjustable baseline burn. This is not a measurement of fat loss, and incomplete logs can make the projection inaccurate.

    Correct with a real weigh-in

    Without a weigh-in, today’s Steps & Health weight is only a starting assumption, not a past measurement. Record a real weigh-in whenever available to reset the forecast. Weigh-ins stay editable by changing the date and value and saving again.
